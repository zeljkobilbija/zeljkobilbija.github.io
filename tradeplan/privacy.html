Trade Plan – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 November 2026 · App: Trade Plan (package com.zeljkobilbija001.tradeplan), developer Željko Bilbija

Summary

The app has no user accounts. The developer does not collect, receive, store or sell any personal data. Everything you enter (API keys, stock symbols, settings, trade journal) is stored only on your phone and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Your API keys and market data

To load market data you enter your own Alpaca API keys (and optionally your own Finnhub key for earnings dates). The keys are stored only on your phone. The app sends them directly to Alpaca (alpaca.markets) or Finnhub (finnhub.io) to request data for the stock symbols you choose – never to the developer. These providers process the requests under their own privacy policies. Using these services and keys is your decision and your responsibility.

Public economic data

Economic reports are downloaded from public sources (U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics – bls.gov, Federal Reserve – federalreserve.gov) without any personal data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads from Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC). To serve and measure ads, Google may process device information such as the advertising ID, IP address, device model and app interactions, and diagnostic data. In the EU/EEA, the UK and Switzerland the app asks for your consent with Google's consent form before ads are loaded; you can change your choice at any time in the menu (Privacy settings). See how Google uses information from apps and the Google Privacy Policy. The Google libraries in the app may process limited account-related information (such as an email address linked to your Google account) for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention under Google's policy; the app itself never reads your email.

In-app purchase “Remove ads”

The optional one-time purchase is handled by Google Play Billing. Payment details are processed only by Google; the app only receives confirmation of the purchase.

Notifications and background monitoring

If you allow notifications, the app shows reminders about economic reports and plans. Background monitoring runs only after you start it, shows a permanent notification and stops automatically after the market closes. No location is used.

Children

The app is intended for adults (18+) and is not directed at children.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability, and to complain to a supervisory authority. Because the developer holds no data about you, requests about advertising data are best directed to Google; you may also contact us.
